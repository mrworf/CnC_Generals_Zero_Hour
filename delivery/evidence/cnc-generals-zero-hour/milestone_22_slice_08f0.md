# M22 08F0: generated selector post-parser boundary

The independent `ZH_M22_GENERATED_SCENE_ROUTE` selector admits only a bounded
mission/skirmish source scenario with the original display factory, Recording
device, and both accepted 08D config/reset selectors. Without it, the 08D
active-water reset retains its exact outer pending stop. With it, the F0A
native update-owned second phase borrows the live display scene/assets instead
of republishing or deleting them. An exact pre-map tick retains the detached
active-water owner and submits no display frame. The source runs
`GameLogic::loadMapINI`, including the 08F0B native smart purge, then emits a
fixed marker and fails closed before terrain load. This is not a scene, frame,
pixel, audio, or retail-fidelity result.

The owned, read-only generated mission/skirmish fixture passes through that
boundary in two independent generations each. It verifies one post-parser
marker, 08D reset marker, typed failure, zero published graphics owners and
Recording resources, and byte-identical source files afterward. Negative
controls cover omitted scene, config, or reset selector; forced device failure;
and missing source provider. Separate ordinary mission/skirmish controls
complete without any new selector. A source-order check requires the marker
between `loadMapINI` and terrain load. No retail roots, providers, private
identifiers, paths, bytes, hashes, or raw process output were used or recorded.
